# CoolClaude

**CoolClaude** is a browser extension that lets you customize the background color of [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).

## Why?

Because bots like drip, too! And, humans can get their customization fix. It's a perfect match.


## What It Does

CoolClaude lets you change the background color of the Claude interface. Features include:

- **HSV color picker** for precise color selection
- **10 preset slots** to save and quickly switch between your favorite colors
- **Per-tab colors** so each Claude conversation can have its own look
- **Persistent preferences** saved locally — your colors are there when you come back
- **Zero data collection** — everything stays on your device

The extension only modifies background colors. It never reads your conversations, tracks your activity, or sends any data anywhere.
