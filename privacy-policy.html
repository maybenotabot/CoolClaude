CoolClaude Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Summary: CoolClaude does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All preferences are stored locally on your device.

What CoolClaude Does

CoolClaude is a browser extension that lets you customize the background color of claude.ai. The extension:

  • Injects CSS to change background color variables on claude.ai
  • Stores your color preferences locally using Chrome's sync storage
  • Uses a MutationObserver to maintain colors when the page updates

What CoolClaude Does NOT Do

  • Does not read, collect, or access any of your conversations or messages
  • Does not track your usage or behavior on claude.ai
  • Does not send any data to external servers
  • Does not modify any content other than background colors
  • Does not include any analytics, telemetry, or tracking code
  • Does not access any personal information

Permissions Explained

  • storage — Saves your color preferences locally on your device
  • activeTab — Allows applying colors to the current claude.ai tab
  • host_permissions (claude.ai) — Required to inject the color-changing CSS

Data Storage

Your color preferences and saved presets are stored locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API. This data:

  • Stays on your device (or syncs across your own Chrome instances if you're signed in)
  • Is never sent to CoolClaude developers or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time by removing the extension

Third-Party Services

CoolClaude does not use any third-party services, analytics platforms, or external APIs.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the project's repository or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.

CoolClaude is open source. You can inspect all code to verify these claims.
